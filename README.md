# Frideswide Ormsby

I am a business content writer, newsletter writer and market research analyst based in Newcastle upon Tyne. I turn detailed notes into readable explainers and help shape publishing plans around practical reader questions.

My work connects four interests: clear business writing, thoughtful blog planning, concise newsletters and careful research. I keep source notes beside my drafts, compare the limits of the available evidence and look for the questions that deserve a conversation.

Away from the desk, I visit community libraries, sketch street corners and take long walks with a notebook. Those everyday observations often give me a better starting point than an empty screen.

I use this space for notes and small projects related to writing, research and digital publishing.
